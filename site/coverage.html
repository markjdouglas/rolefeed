What this feed cannot see

An employer that is silently absent looks exactly like an employer with no vacancies. That is the most dangerous failure mode here, because it reads as a quiet market rather than as a blind spot. This page exists so the blind spot is a measured number rather than an assumption.

Unreachable employers

No open endpoint found under any of the six supported systems. Most run SuccessFactors, Taleo or iCIMS; some are on a system not yet probed, which is the cheapest thing left to fix.
