Why the hypothesis failed

This page is the evidence. Two thirds of the employers on the list cannot be reached at all, the shortfall concentrates on the largest ones, and no amount of adding companies changes that — which is why development stopped. The product brief sets out the decision.

What this feed can and cannot see

An employer that is silently absent looks exactly like an employer with no vacancies, which reads as a quiet market rather than a blind spot. Naming the absences is the one thing here that no job board will do for you.
