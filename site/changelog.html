Version history

v0.4.2 current

21/09/2026
  • Coverage page added: all 211 employers, reachable or not, by size band and by system.
  • Feed now defaults to the full in-scope set. Stats lead with roles assessed, then in scope, then matched.
  • Every role shows why it matched: seniority tier, which geography gate passed, and whether the date came from the employer.
  • Navigation moved to a top bar, in an identical position on all four pages.
  • Product brief and version history widened to the feed's 1,180px column, with the same gutters.
  • Product brief cut by roughly 40%; heading sizes made hierarchical.
  • Banner pluralisation fixed — “1 employers unavailable”.
  • Header compacted. The brand lockup moved into the top bar, so three stacked bands became one. Pixel scale halved from 2.6 to 1.6, header padding 22px to 10px, meter 7px to 4px.
  • Nine stat cards became one 34px strip — values beside labels rather than above them. The cards cost about 130 vertical pixels and pushed the first role below the fold on a 13-inch screen.
  • One control height, padding and type size for tabs, selects, sort and toggles. They had been 36px, 38px and 34px, with 2px, 0 and 2px shadows and two border widths, sitting on the same rows.
  • Search, selects, sort and toggles share one wrapping row above 640px, down from three rows.
  • A single 2px boundary now separates the controls from the content, replacing four weak separators that read as drift.
  • “Matched only” restored as the default. Turning it off to make the funnel visible was the wrong fix — the feed opened on a data-science role. The funnel belongs in the stats strip, which is where it now is.
  • Title exclusions extended to finance, financial, legal, payroll, procurement, talent and HR operations. Those were only excluded when followed by manager, director or lead, so “Finance Operations Lead” passed the domain test on the word “operations” and was caught by the mid-senior tier. Verified against 17 titles.
  • Fixed a duplicated line in the publish loop that appended every role to the feed twice. 1,128 published roles were 564 real ones. It threw no error and the totals looked plausible, which is why it survived a run.
  • The geography verdict is now stored on each role. It was computed and discarded, so every role shipped with no location classification and the page could not distinguish a UK role from “remote, region unstated”.
  • SmartRecruiters paging verified in production: Sixt 100 to about 510 roles, Delivery Hero 100 to about 990, Grab 100 to about 420. Total supply 3,698 to about 5,500.

v0.4.1

21/09/2026
  • Board discovery re-run: 62 of 211 employers resolved (29%, from 22%), 3,918 open roles, 23.3 minutes.
  • Reachability by size: enterprise 22%, large 51%, mid 35%, scaleup 18%, startup 17%.
  • 31% of reachable enterprise employers are on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby, against 80% large, 89% mid, 89% scaleup, 100% startup.
  • SmartRecruiters paging added. Sixt, Delivery Hero and Grab were each capped at exactly 100 postings.
  • Job URLs restricted to http and https. Escaping alone does not stop a javascript: link.
  • noindex and robots.txt added. Neither is access control.
  • Checkerboard moved to the page gutters; at 9% alpha it had been sitting behind body text.
  • Cron moved from :00 to :37 after firing 11–17 minutes late on 14 of 14 runs.
  • CLAUDE.md, README.md and SPEC.md corrected. All three still described a Google Sheet as the interface.
  • Goose interval 7s to 5s.

v0.4.0

21/09/2026
  • Teamtailor added as a sixth adapter: 8 employers, including GXO, Maersk, Geodis and Europcar.
  • Recency uses the employer's publication date where the source provides one. Greenhouse's updated_at is not one — it moves on any edit, which is why the 24h, 48h and 7d counts had been identical.
  • Workday relative dates parsed (“Posted 3 Days Ago”).
  • Seniority split into exec, director and mid-senior. Verified against 39 sample titles.
  • Employer list 169 to 211, with a startup band added.
  • British Airways assessed and recorded as uncovered: no machine-readable feed, and robots.txt disallows the search path.
  • Goose gained three frames and 15 weighted acts.

v0.3.0

20/09/2026
  • Geography restricted to the UK, or remote within GMT ±3. Impostor guard rejects London Ontario, Birmingham Alabama and Manchester New Hampshire.
  • Hot filter for roles under 24 hours old.
  • Live countdown to the next collection on a 24-block meter, to the second.
  • Mobile filters collapsed from over half the viewport to 86px.
  • Mobile Safari: 16px inputs, safe-area insets, dvh units, 44px touch targets.

v0.2.0

20/09/2026
  • Web interface: segmented by employer size, faceted filters, shortlist, dismiss. One file, no framework, no build step.
  • Deployed on Cloudflare Pages from a private repository. GitHub Pro proved unnecessary.
  • State merge so first_seen survives each run. Roles missing twice are treated as closed.
  • Scheduled collection every two hours via GitHub Actions, committing only on change.
  • /site found in the stock Python .gitignore, silently excluding the entire application.

v0.1.0

19/09/2026
  • First collector: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters. All keyless.
  • Board-token discovery by variant generation. Greenhouse's separate EU host added.
  • Workday adapter, hand-configured per employer from a careers URL.
  • First pass resolved 25% of employers.
Versioning is major.minor.patch, bumped by hand. Still 0.x: the data contract is not stable and the matching rules are being tuned. Reasoning for each change lives in the product brief.
